OUR SPECIALITIES

Be Smart Driving Academy delivers quality state-approved novice driver education, certified 3rd-party DMV road testing, and specialized winter ice safety training to students and families across Anchorage, Eagle River, and the greater Mat-Su area. We keep pricing upfront and scheduling simple, so your driving journey stays on track from day one.

16-HOUR NOVICE DRIVER COURSE

State of Alaska approved complete curriculum for first-time teen and adult drivers. From 8 hours of in-depth classroom safety theory to 8 hours of 1-on-1 dual-control in-car driving with Butch Evans, plus your official State DMV Road Test included with up to 40% insurance discount.

DMV THIRD-PARTY ROAD TESTS

Skip weeks of state DMV wait lines and get your official Alaska driver's license on your schedule. We administer certified third-party road tests in a calm, stress-free environment using our dual-control vehicles or your own, with same-day paperwork filed directly with the DMV.

ALASKA WINTER DRIVING SAFETY

Master Alaska's most treacherous winter roads with confidence. Specialized behind-the-wheel instruction covering black ice skid recovery, 8–10 second following distance rules, threshold braking, and emergency survival tactics. Essential for new Alaska residents and JBER military.

DEFENSIVE DRIVING & DIP

Alaska court-approved Driver Improvement Program (DIP). Clear moving violation demerit points from your official driving record, satisfy court requirements, and lower your annual auto insurance premium rates with our certified single-day intensive course.

PRIVATE 1-ON-1 LESSONS

Customized behind-the-wheel coaching tailored for nervous teens and adult beginners. Focus on parallel parking, highway merging, multi-lane roundabouts, and confidence building with veteran instructor Butch Evans at your personal pace.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Straight answers from Head Instructor Butch Evans about class registration, road tests, vehicle dual controls, and teen requirements.

How do I register for a class or road test?

Fill out the fast callback form on this page or call Butch directly at (907) 868-3151. Butch personally contacts you within 15–30 minutes during business hours to confirm availability and lock in your schedule.

Does submitting the form require immediate payment?

No deposit required. Submitting the form reserves your preferred date and program with zero upfront charge. You pay only after we confirm availability and lock in your exact calendar schedule with you directly.

What if the student is under 18 years old?

Students under 18 require parental or legal guardian consent. Please provide parent contact details when requesting your slot. A parent or guardian signs the enrollment agreement prior to behind-the-wheel driving lessons.

What should students bring to their first lesson?

Please bring a valid Alaska learner's permit or government photo ID, weather-appropriate clothing for Alaska conditions, and comfortable closed-toe driving shoes (avoid heavy winter boots or loose flip-flops while operating vehicle pedals).

How does the Third-Party DMV Road Test work?

As a State-Certified third-party tester, we administer the official State of Alaska road test on our schedule so you don't have to wait 4 to 6 weeks at the state DMV. Once you pass, your results are immediately certified to collect your permanent Alaska driver's license.

How does the up to 40% insurance discount work?

Graduating from our State of Alaska Approved 16-Hour Novice Course or Defensive Driving Program awards an official certificate that major insurers (State Farm, GEICO, Progressive, USAA, Allstate) accept for premium discounts of up to 40% for up to 3 years.
